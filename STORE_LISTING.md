## Long description

```
The lantern is lit, the ink is warm, and the rain is falling. Welcome to
your little cartography shop — open only after dark.

By night, adventurers slip in from the storm: weary knights, scheming
merchants, wandering bards, and the occasional polite monster. Each one
needs a map drawn from the clues they remember — a forest here, a river
there, ruins half-swallowed by the swamp. Listen closely, place your tiles,
and hand over your best guess of a world no one has fully seen.

The closer your map matches the truth, the better your reward and the
brighter your reputation. Earn gold, unlock cozy upgrades for the shop,
and uncover fragments of lore about the land you keep redrawing, night
after night.

There are no timers, no punishments, no rush. Just a warm desk, a quiet
ritual, and the small satisfaction of approximating the truth one tile at
a time.

FEATURES
• Draw maps from memory — listen to adventurers’ clues and place tiles
  on a blank grid before the night is over
• Six types of customers: knights, mages, merchants, rangers, bards, and
  the occasional monster with surprisingly good directions
• Accuracy matters — the closer your map to the truth, the bigger the
  reward and the better your reputation in town
• Upgrade your shop between nights: enchanted ink, extra clues, cozy
  decor, and a reputation shield for when the map is... approximate
• Tiles for every terrain — forests, rivers, mountains, deserts, ruins,
  villages, dungeons, swamps, and the open sea
• Uncover a quiet story one lore fragment at a time as nights pass and
  regulars return to your desk
• Candle-lit, rain-soaked atmosphere with a calm, unhurried pace
• No timers, no fail states, no ads — pick it up, put it down, return
  whenever you want something gentle
• Fully playable offline — no account, no sign-in, no internet required
• Optional “Tip the Owl” support — leave a coin if you like the shop;
  supporters can flip back through earlier clues mid-contract

FREE TO PLAY, NO ADS
The game is completely free with no advertisements. If you enjoy your
nights at the shop, you can leave an optional “Tip the Owl” to support
development. Supporters get a small thank-you and the ability to flip back
through earlier clues during a contract. That’s it — no paywalls, no
pressure.

Pour a cup of something warm. The maps won’t draw themselves.
```
