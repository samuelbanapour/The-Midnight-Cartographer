Free, with no strings

The whole game is free and ad-free. If you enjoy your nights, you can leave an optional tip.

Drop a coin in the jar with “Tip the Owl” — $1, $2.99, $5, or $10 — and you become a supporter. As a small thank-you, supporters can flip back through earlier clues mid-contract. That’s the only difference. No paywalls, no pressure.

Questions from the doorway

 

What devices does it run on?

The Midnight Cartographer is built for Amazon Fire tablets and available on the Amazon Appstore. It plays entirely offline once installed.

How much does it cost?

It’s free. There’s an optional “Tip the Owl” in-app purchase if you’d like to support development, but the full game is playable without spending anything.

Are there ads?

No ads, ever. No timers, no energy meters, no fail states either.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account and no sign-in. Internet is only used for the optional tip purchase and restore — the game itself runs fully offline.

Where can I get it?

It’s available now on the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets. Tap the button at the top of the page or search “The Midnight Cartographer” in the Appstore.
